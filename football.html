Football

Introduction

Football is one of the most popular sports in the world. Two teams of eleven players try to score goals by moving the ball into the opposing team's goal.

Basic Rules

  • Each team normally has eleven players on the field.
  • Players use their feet and other permitted body parts to control the ball.
  • Only the goalkeeper may handle the ball within their own penalty area, subject to the rules.
  • The team scoring more goals wins the match.

Benefits of Football

  • Improves fitness and coordination.
  • Develops teamwork and communication.
  • Builds confidence and discipline.
