Basketball

Introduction

Basketball is a team sport played by two teams. Players try to score points by throwing the ball through the opposing team's basket.

Basic Rules

  • Players move the ball by passing or dribbling.
  • Players cannot run while holding the ball without dribbling.
  • Teams score points by shooting the ball into the basket.
  • The team with the most points wins.

Benefits of Basketball

  • Improves speed and coordination.
  • Develops teamwork and quick decision-making.
  • Helps improve physical fitness.
