Athletics

Introduction

Athletics includes running, jumping, and throwing events. It is an important part of many school sports competitions and international events.

Types of Athletics

  • Sprinting and distance running
  • Relay races
  • Long jump and high jump
  • Shot put and javelin throw

Benefits of Athletics

  • Improves endurance and coordination.
  • Develops discipline and concentration.
  • Encourages personal improvement and confidence.
