Cricket

Introduction

Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport played between two teams. One team bats to score runs while the other team bowls and fields to limit runs and take wickets.

Basic Rules

  • Teams take turns batting and bowling.
  • Batters score runs by running between the wickets or hitting boundaries.
  • Bowlers try to dismiss the batters.
  • The team with the better score wins, according to the match rules.

Cricket Equipment

  • Cricket bat
  • Cricket ball
  • Wickets and stumps
  • Batting pads, gloves, and other protective equipment
